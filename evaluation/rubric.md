# Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Incorrect, misleading, or clearly inappropriate |
| 2 | Major problems; partially useful |
| 3 | Acceptable but noticeably imperfect |
| 4 | Strong response with minor issues |
| 5 | Highly accurate, natural, contextually appropriate |

## Dimensions

1. **Dialect authenticity** — sounds appropriate for the requested Yemeni context rather than generic MSA or an unrelated dialect.
2. **Semantic accuracy** — preserves the intended meaning.
3. **Contextual understanding** — interprets the surrounding situation correctly.
4. **Cultural relevance** — avoids culturally inappropriate assumptions and handles pragmatic meaning correctly.
5. **Naturalness** — reads like natural human language in the intended setting.
6. **Grammar / morphology** — appropriate grammar, agreement, morphology, and word choice.
7. **Intent preservation** — preserves what the speaker is trying to accomplish.

## Failure categories

- `DIALECT_MISMATCH`
- `MSA_OVERUSE`
- `CULTURAL_MISMATCH`
- `INTENT_ERROR`
- `LITERAL_TRANSLATION`
- `UNNATURAL_PHRASE`
- `GRAMMAR_ERROR`
- `UNSAFE_OR_INAPPROPRIATE`
